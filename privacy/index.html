Privacy Policy

Effective: September 13, 2026 · 生效日期：2026年9月13日

English

VoltCluster is designed to process vehicle information primarily on your device. We do not sell personal information and currently do not use third-party advertising, tracking, or analytics SDKs.

Information processed

  • Vehicle information you provide or receive from a connected vehicle, such as vehicle profile, VIN, telemetry snapshots, Bluetooth connection state, trip or navigation state, diagnostic information, and app preferences.
  • Purchase and subscription status provided by Apple through StoreKit.
  • Location-related information only when you grant permission and use features that require it. Map features may use Apple MapKit and are subject to Apple's privacy practices.

How information is used

Information is used to display the dashboard, connect to compatible vehicle hardware, provide navigation and widgets, restore settings, diagnose issues, and determine access to subscription features.

Storage and sharing

App preferences and vehicle data are stored locally on your device unless a feature explicitly tells you otherwise. Subscription transactions are handled by Apple. We do not operate an account or analytics server for the current version. Information may be disclosed only when legally required or necessary to protect safety and rights.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you remove it in the app, delete the app, or erase the device. The app provides a way to delete locally stored data. Apple retains purchase records under its own policies.

Permissions and choices

You can control Bluetooth, Location, Notifications, and other permissions in iOS Settings. Some features will not work without the related permission. You can manage subscriptions in your Apple Account settings.

Children

VoltCluster is not directed to children under 13 and is intended for licensed drivers or passengers using the app safely.

Security and driving safety

We use reasonable safeguards, but no system is completely secure. Do not interact with the app while driving. Always follow road laws and rely on the vehicle's official controls and warnings.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will revise the effective date. Questions: joyhong520@gmail.com.

简体中文

VoltCluster 主要在您的设备本地处理车辆信息。我们不会出售个人信息，当前版本也不使用第三方广告、追踪或分析 SDK。

处理的信息

  • 您提供或从已连接车辆取得的信息，例如车辆资料、VIN、车辆信号快照、蓝牙连接状态、行程或导航状态、诊断信息和应用偏好设置。
  • Apple 通过 StoreKit 提供的购买与订阅状态。
  • 仅在您授权并使用相关功能时处理位置信息。地图功能可能使用 Apple MapKit，并适用 Apple 的隐私规则。

用途、存储与共享

这些信息用于显示仪表、连接兼容车辆硬件、提供导航和 Widget、恢复设置、诊断问题以及判断订阅功能权限。当前版本不运营账户或分析服务器；应用偏好和车辆数据保存在设备本地，订阅交易由 Apple 处理。除法律要求或保护安全与权利所必需外，我们不会披露信息。

保留、删除与权限

本地数据会保留到您在应用内删除、卸载应用或抹掉设备。您可以在应用内删除本地数据，并在 iOS“设置”中管理蓝牙、位置、通知等权限。Apple 会依据其政策保留购买记录。

儿童与安全

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童，适合持证驾驶员或乘客安全使用。驾驶时请勿操作应用，并始终遵守交通法规及车辆官方控制和警告。

政策更新与联系
