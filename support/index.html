Support / 技术支持

Need help with VoltCluster? / 需要帮助？

Contact / 联系方式

Email: joyhong520@gmail.com

For non-sensitive bug reports, you can also use GitHub Issues. Please do not post VINs, precise locations, purchase receipts, or other private data publicly.

非敏感问题也可提交至 GitHub Issues。请勿公开发布 VIN、精确位置、购买凭证或其他隐私信息。

Subscriptions / 订阅

Use “Restore Purchases” in the app if an active purchase is not recognized. Manage or cancel subscriptions in iOS Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions. / 若有效订阅未被识别，请在应用内选择“恢复购买”；管理或取消订阅请前往 iOS“设置”→ Apple 账户 →“订阅”。

Vehicle connection / 车辆连接

Confirm Bluetooth permission, compatible hardware, and that the app is in range. Do not troubleshoot while driving. / 请确认蓝牙权限、硬件兼容性及连接距离。请勿在驾驶时排查问题。

Delete local data / 删除本地数据

Use the delete-data option in app settings, or delete the app from your device. / 可在应用设置中删除本地数据，或从设备卸载应用。

Typical response target: 3–5 business days. / 通常会在 3–5 个工作日内回复。
